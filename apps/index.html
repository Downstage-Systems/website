Downstage Show Builder

Lay out your Bitfocus Companion pages on a picture of your Stream Deck, then send them straight to Companion. A free Mac app.

Download for Mac See it on GitHub

Free. Apple Silicon Macs. Needs Bitfocus Companion.

Why it exists

"As a Blackmagic ATEM user, I got tired of rebuilding the same multi-step, feedback-driven buttons every time a show's sources changed."
Rob Spradling, Downstage Systems

Build the page once.

It doesn't replace Companion. That's still where you set up connections and fine-tune actions and feedbacks. Show Builder handles the repetitive part.

Recipes

What happens when you press a source, with the source left as a blank slot. Build it once, and it works on any input.

Straight to Companion

Send a page directly onto a Companion page, with no fiddling in the import and export screen. Or export a page file.

Every deck, side by side

Lay out several Stream Decks in one window, and a Home page builds its own navigation. Works with the + XL, +, XL, 15 key, Neo and Mini, or a custom grid.

Saved shows

Keep every deck and page for a gig in one file. Next gig, open it, change what's different and send it again.

Ready-made keys

Flying keys, T-bar dials, SuperSource boxes, tally keys, combo keys and live status like on-air and stream time, plus a wizard for building new ones. The Connections tab brings in the presets from the rest of your gear.

Your first page in about five minutes.

Install

Unzip it and drag it to Applications. macOS blocks it the first time: go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.

Connect

Type your Companion's address in the header. Your switcher's source names appear.

Build

Pick a recipe, click a source, click a key. Then choose Send to Companion and Build page.
