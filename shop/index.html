Questions about ordering.

When can I buy one?
Now. Pre-orders are open, and the first units ship in January 2027. You pay when you order, and you can cancel any time before your order ships for a full refund. See the kits.
What if it isn't right for my show?
Cancel a pre-order any time before it ships for a full refund. Once it arrives, you have 30 days to send it back in working condition for a refund; you pay the shipping on a return. Every light is covered by our one-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship; if one fails, we repair or replace it, and we send you a prepaid label to ship it to us. Email hello@downstage.systems.
What powers them?
USB-C, on all three: a USB-C charger or a power bank. Some camera USB ports cannot supply enough power, so check yours before the show. USB power is how the lights run a show. The Downstage Cue and the Cue Lite have a built-in backup battery, about 2 hours and about 1 h 20 min away from power; no light lasts a show day on battery. The Cue 360 runs on USB power only.

More questions? Support has setup, CueLink and troubleshooting.

Built by hand, one at a time.

Every Downstage unit is assembled, flashed and tested by hand by Rob Spradling in Littleton, Colorado. Designed on shows across the country, and built in small batches so each one is checked before it ships.
